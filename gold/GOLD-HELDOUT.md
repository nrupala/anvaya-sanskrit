# Held-out gold — hand derivation

Source: Pañcatantra, Tantra 1 (Mitrabheda), Wikisource edition
"पञ्चतन्त्रम् ०२" (sa.wikisource.org), read 2026-11-19. A different
text from the fixture (Kathāmukha): the chain was built and tuned on
the fixture only; nothing here was seen during the build. Sentences
chosen to cover the kārakas the fixture lacks — tṛtīyā (karaṇa) is
absent here too in the end (see the note at H5), caturthī
(sampradāna, tādarthya) at H5, pañcamī (apādāna) at H4 — and to
exercise absolutive chains, the name device on new names, and
participle adjectives. Edition notes: H5 is printed "तच् च" in this
edition (halant spacing); the sentences file writes तच्च, the form
every other edition prints. H4 prints पिपासाकुल without its visarga
before उदक- (elision in the edition itself).

Derivation discipline as the spike's GOLD.md: every relation from
the case on the word (vibhakti → kāraka, Aṣṭādhyāyī citations),
agreement (sāmānādhikaraṇya) for viśeṣaṇa, the absolutive rule
(3.4.21), the closed-class devices (नाम, च, अथ, तत्र). Relations the
reader's grammar cannot express are golded TRUE and flagged; their
misses are named in advance, not discovered after the fact:

- pronominal adjectives (H2 तत्, H2 तम्, H5 तत्): golded viśeṣaṇa
  by agreement. The reader's flat case grammar reads an oblique
  pronoun as a second karma and a nominative one as an apposition.
  Expected misses: 3.
- participle government (H4 यमुनातटम् → अवतीर्णः): a kta participle
  governs its own karma; the reader's predicates are only the
  finite verb and the absolutives. Expected miss: 1.
- purpose cases (H4 उदकपानार्थम् karma; H5 अर्थागमाय sampradāna):
  the prayojana accusative (2.3.2) and tādarthya dative (2.3.14)
  subsumed under the reader's only class for their vibhakti.
  Convention-flagged, expected to score.

## H1 — तत्र धर्मोपार्जितभूरिविभवो वर्धमानको नाम वणिक्पुत्रो बभूव।

- h1 धर्मोपार्जितभूरिविभवः → वणिक्पुत्रः, viśeṣaṇa — bahuvrīhi,

- h2 वर्धमानकः → वणिक्पुत्रः, name-apposition — flagged by नाम.
